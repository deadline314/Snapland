# World-space avatar gait

The island uses this rig for the hero’s eight-direction walk, jog and idle. Authored seated/action sprites remain separate. Existing first 3D and earlier prototype images remain negative/reference evidence; they are not the delivered gait.

The source provider obtains immutable normalized renderer walk_idle_frame art for each hero direction. Explicit per-direction ownership removes only original leg skin, adjacent outline and complete boot boxes; source head, face, hair, camera, bag and shorts pixels remain. Metadata records actual opaque shorts openings separately for each hip, original skin texture and complete boot crops. Missing or invalid parts log their path and fall back to the untouched original sprite; fallback does not count as rig coverage.

The NPC/default synthetic rig uses nominal 0.225 m upper and lower bones at scale 1.0; role-scaled NPC fixtures proportionally scale that legacy chain. Production Hero instead measures a median source-pivot chain of 0.374064327 m and splits it into two fixed ~0.187032164 m bones. A continuous tapered eight-sided source-skin tube follows that chain. A curved knee centerline avoids cap outlines, with inside radius limited to avoid folded triangles. Per-frame rendering reuses two allocated vertex byte buffers. Complete original forward footwear is reused for the hidden rear leg when its original shoe faces backwards; no whole character mirroring. The isolated generated back heel is an explicitly distinct candidate: actual alpha region, ankle and sole are calibrated, and warm leather RGB ratios are matched to original right-shoe leather at initialization while preserving alpha/navy outline. The saved generated asset remains unchanged. Earlier candidate-five captures are historical review evidence, not acceptance of the current Hero appearance set.

World feet lock during stance; actual movement distance drives phase. Touchdown epochs avoid stale contacts after skipped cycles. Pelvis motion and per-leg openings move hips without stretching bones. Stops preserve planted XZ, settle swing feet and breathe. Upper art and its hip offsets use the same camera projection basis. The fixture defaults to world orthographic offset (3.5,16.5,25), target height 1; C toggles portrait projection. Boots remain vertical at camera yaw with measured strong sole on floor. Upper attachments share rigid lean/bob, never face/hair deformation.

Canonical scale is 2.4/360 m per source pixel. The hero walk uses a 0.36 m foot stroke and 0.6 stance, giving a 0.600 m full cycle; its peak lift is 35 mm. At 0.55 m/s this is about 0.92 cycles/s (1.83 steps/s). Source-measured boot-roll pivots and limits are used only for the hero left/right views. Run uses the 0.28 m stroke and 0.2 stance for a 1.4 m cycle; 2 m/s gives about 1.43 cycles/s, flight and lift rather than stretched legs. The island now defaults to 2 m/s jog with Shift selecting 0.55 m/s careful walk; its acceleration and deceleration last 0.16 seconds. The review fixture’s independent acceleration remains 2.5 m/s/s; actual island movement, rather than requested velocity, drives the production phase. Direction/mode changes reset contacts and blend.

CPU checks cover eight directions, portrait/world projection, walk/run, two legs and 256 phases: fixed bones/reach, world plant/sole, actual strong-alpha shorts overlap with the skin ring, nonzero triangles, noncollapsed width, whole source boot pixels, exact face and untouched garment pixels. Actual advance() tests verify contact locking and stopping. Missing-metadata fallback, cancellation, cleanup and bounded resources are also checked. A real `_ready` regression injects one invalid Hero source direction, verifies complete original-source fallback for all directions, then rebuilds and verifies recovery. CPU checks do not establish visual acceptance.

Native scene: game/tests/native_avatar_motion_rig_review.tscn. --capture-motion-rig saves the versioned candidate8 static phase PNGs then performs actual advance() through two cycles per direction/mode, stops and bob/elf plus curly/imp appearance changes. The idle review also captures breath, steady and camera-check phases for all three looks and eight directions. Continuous footage must be recorded externally; this scene does not encode video. --continuous-only skips snapshots; --capture-static-only skips motion; --quarter-motion-rig sets quarter simulation speed; --portrait-motion-rig selects portrait camera. Header distinguishes static/continuous and shows actual speed, phase, distance and time scale. Existing negative image filenames are not overwritten. Idle9's front/front-left transparent eye/nose band was a historical rejection. Idle10's 72 PNG captures and focused native front-bob/curly-back-right views were visually reviewed with that band absent. This is a limited appearance/frame review; it does not establish natural motion across every action or appearance. Full multi-appearance acceptance remains pending.

Round-three correction: source garment preservation uses a flood-connected cream mask seeded above the leg ownership region, plus its immediate dark outline. Disconnected cream shoe highlights are removed with all other legacy footwear. Each hip has a measured source opening span and half width. A source-aligned proximal tube collar spans that opening, rests behind the real garment plane, and blends into the physical chain below the hem. Whole-width projected coverage and at least four opaque garment contacts are required at every sampled phase; all legacy boot ownership pixels outside the garment mask must be transparent.

All upper and boot Sprite3D components use shaded=true and no_depth_test=false; skin materials use per-pixel shading with normal depth. Pose-derived surface normals use neighboring ring/axial vertices. Fixed byte buffers update vertex positions and octahedral normal/tangent data together, following the official [RenderingServer layout](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/master/servers/rendering/rendering_server.cpp) and [Vector3 encoding](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/master/core/math/vector3.cpp). CPU verifies unit normals and decoded uploaded-byte agreement. No depth bypass or unlit material hides joints. Native L toggles day/night key and ambient light; W cycles wall off/full/opening. --lighting-motion-rig uses night light for run samples; --occlusion-motion-rig enables the wall opening. These are GPU review aids, not a claim that native occlusion or lighting has passed.

## Role-specific gait measurements

The rig now accepts `metadata_path`, `required_directions`, and `leg_length_scale` before it enters the scene tree. Hero defaults remain the original anchor metadata, all eight directions, and scale `1.0`. A role must list only directions for which it has measured metadata and a source-provider result; the default eight-direction table is not evidence that an NPC supports eight views. Scale is constrained to `0.35..1.2` and proportionally adjusts both fixed bone lengths, mesh rows/radii and bend transition, hip height, walk/run foot stroke and lift, ankle/boot dimensions, and world-space sole depth. Static geometry/IK helpers take optional length parameters whose defaults retain the hero values.

Complete normalized-source measurements may override the role-scaled geometry when a direction entry includes `measurements.boot_or_bare_foot_rect_complete`, `measurements.ankle_height_source_m`, and `measurements.sole_row_px`, plus its measured `pelvis` row. The source hip height is `(sole_row_px - pelvis_y) * PIXEL_SIZE`; the ankle height comes directly from `ankle_height_source_m`. Measured boot pixel size and `hip_half_width_px` use the normalized source scale once, rather than being multiplied by `leg_length_scale` again. The solver may lower a nominal measured hip to fit the two fixed bone reaches; it does not lengthen either bone. For example, the ticket fixture uses rows 346/260 for a 0.573 m hip height and 0.333 m ankle, while the announcer uses rows 346/226 for a 0.800 m hip and 0.267 m ankle. Entries without complete source measurements—including the default synthetic hero fixtures—retain the previous proportional ankle, boot, hip-height, and hip-width behavior.

The CPU role regression uses a synthetic provider backed by the renderer's unchanged source composite and a uniquely named role metadata file under the operating-system temporary directory, containing only measured left/right entries. It checks shorter fixed bone lengths, exact source appearance/upper garment pixels, both an upward and downward ground-height change, and rendered boot soles against the sampled ground. A role absent from those measurements remains an invalid original-source fallback with the configured metadata path in its diagnostic. This validates scale plumbing and fallback behavior only; it does not establish native visual acceptance or complete NPC source/art coverage.

Role entries may also declare optional `limb_garment_max_y`. When present, only pixels below that measured row and inside the role's `limb_envelopes` are removed from the connected garment mask; the existing `garment_guards` are then reapplied before connected limb ownership is found. This allows opaque non-skin trouser fabric to continue into the deforming lower-leg mesh without relying on skin hue. Entries without the field, including hero measurements, retain the existing garment-mask behavior byte-for-byte. The CPU fixtures use a connected green trouser source and normalized ticket/announcer dimensions to check exact pixel preservation, unscaled measured boot and hip widths, measured hip/ankle heights, and fixed-length reach solving. NPC roles using a source provider do not instantiate the hero renderer cache.

Measured NPC source widths now set the entire tapered bind tube independently of its fixed bone-length ratio. Each direction caches two bounded bind arrays during source preparation; the cuff uses that same radial unit, so source width is neither reduced a second time nor multiplied twice. Complete measured boot crops retain all source RGBA pixels, including pixels whose hue also matches the exposed-skin classifier. An initialization-only scan locates the actual opaque shaft span at the measured ankle/crop boundary; its horizontal centre replaces a whole-foot centre that may lie in transparent space above the toe. The anatomical ankle retains its measured sole-to-ankle height, while its depth places the distal tube behind the boot plane. The final mesh ring continues into the opaque shaft, covering any gap between the ankle row and the retained crop without moving the sole or changing either bone length. Default hero geometry and source extraction keep their previous path.

The role CPU regression also uses the real immutable NPC provider for three roles, both supported views, two camera projections and 32 gait phases. It checks atlas SHA and source/upper RGBA preservation, complete opaque boot pixels and soles, fixed bones, calf diameter relative to source width, and the final ring's overlap with actual opaque shaft pixels and rendered boot depth. These are geometric and source-integrity checks. The eleventh Main-native review also completed and visually reviewed normal (74.433 s) and quarter-rate (297.683 s) recordings, plus all 21 role/phase images; the reviewed hand/hip cutout defect was absent. That scoped result does not certify natural movement or authored walk-cycle quality.

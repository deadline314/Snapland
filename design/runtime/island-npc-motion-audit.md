## Runtime behavior and limits

`character_animation_library.gd` reports manifest coverage and builds fixed-canvas textures with translated anchors. The current actor entries use the same 320x460 canvas and feet baseline 448, but distinct reference sizes: ticket 130x200, imp 150x180, announcer 160x180. The loader's shared per-action/direction scale and each frame's feet anchor keep feet aligned; retain this frame-space convention when adding actions.

`npc_activity.gd` registers each actor on its own authored front idle and retains the common 320x460 frame-space / feet-anchor convention. Idle events begin after a seed-derived 5–12 seconds; later events are spaced 7–16 seconds and phase-rotated so adjacent actors do not repeatedly select the same first option. Ticket chooses only `idle_gesture` or `emotion_happy`; imp chooses `idle_gesture`, `emotion_proud`, or `dance`; announcer chooses `special` or `emotion_curious`. The immediately previous secondary action is excluded. One-shot actions return to the actor's authored standing loop. All role actions are checked against the loaded animation library; missing clips do not fabricate an animation.

All three NPC roles are enabled in the current island World through the measured left/right source adapter and procedural lower-leg motion. These are turnaround-derived inputs, not authored walk cycles; integrated native visual acceptance remains pending. The announcer’s ±0.6 lateral route now shares an 8.6-second cycle with its two measured side-source directions and procedural lower-leg motion: 1.0 second out, a rest through 3.3, a return through 5.3, a second rest through 7.6, and a return to the route origin. Route keys use linear position interpolation so the endpoint remains stable through the stop instead of cubic overshoot. At each stop, the side source continues for a short 0.16-second procedural settle; then the actor changes to its real authored front idle, followed shortly by a verified personality action. This removes the immediate side-view-to-front-idle snap while preserving the source's limited view coverage. Ticket and imp have short runtime routes, but their turnaround-derived procedural motion is not an authored walk cycle and has not passed integrated native visual acceptance. No actor gets a fabricated back, diagonal, or toward-camera walk.

The scheduler returns early during reduced-motion and paused/modal states, so idle actions do not interrupt dialogue or catch up in a burst afterward. Headless checks now validate each role's authored idle/actions, no immediate secondary-action repeats, announcer settling/front-rest behavior, bounded route movement, reduced-motion restoration, pause/resume, fallback and cleanup. These are CPU/runtime checks only; the new transition timing and idle choices still need native visual review for stride feel and whether the secondary poses read naturally at island camera distance.

## Safe follow-up for NPC adaptation

Preserve each role’s authored front-facing idle, greet, interact, emotion and personality poses. The current World enables all three roles on short routes using measured left/right turnaround-derived inputs and procedural lower-leg motion; do not describe these as authored walk cycles or as eight-direction coverage. For announcer, preserve the two measured side inputs and ground-anchor contract. Any further gait changes need CPU and integrated native review before acceptance is claimed. If adding role actions, update manifest coverage and add per-role tests for direction availability, frame count, non-null textures, head/feet anchor consistency, and loop/one-shot timing. The native scene still needs human visual review for stride feel, ground contact, and transitions; manifest or headless checks alone cannot establish that.

All three roles are enabled by the island World. Their left/right gait inputs come from measured turnaround art and are animated procedurally; they are not authored walk cycles. Front idle, greet, interact, emotion and role-specific secondary poses use the authored action sources. Pause, reduced-motion and dialogue-hold behavior are runtime controls, not evidence of native visual quality. The integrated NPC review is still pending. Hero gait calibration is separate: its walk cycle is 0.600 m with 35 mm peak lift, and source-measured foot-roll applies only to hero left/right views. NPC lift caps remain 18 mm for ticket, 20 mm for imp, and 24 mm for announcer; none inherits the hero foot-roll calibration.
